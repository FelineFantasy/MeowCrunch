# MeowCrunch Game 🐱

[![Python Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.10%2B-blue.svg)](https://python.org)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green.svg)](LICENSE)

A text-based farm simulator written in Python. Play as Lao Zhang, manage a unique plantation of "MeowCrunch" cats, grow valuable golden crusts, and protect your harvest!

## 📝 Description

**MeowCrunch** is a console simulation game where you manage a very unusual farm. Your main asset is a special breed of cats. They grow unique golden crusts on their heads which mature over 90 days to be turned into world-famous delicacies. 

The path to a perfect harvest is full of challenges, as you must balance several core resources:
- **Satiety** — well-fed cats grow better crusts.
- **Happiness** — if mood drops, the quality of the harvest plummets.
- **Energy** — required for training, playing, and working.
- **Health** — overall condition that sets the baseline for the crop.
- **Crust Quality** — the final parameters of your harvest, improved via training and care.
- **Coins** — farm currency used to buy food, treats, and vet services.

**Conditions:**
- 🏆 **Victory**: Survive 90 days and harvest a perfect batch of golden crusts (Quality > 80)!
- 💀 **Defeat**: If Satiety, Happiness, or Health drops to 0, your cats run away or perish, resulting in Game Over.

### ⚠️ Farm Hazards & Events
Random encounters happen every day. Watch out for **loud noises** that scare the cats (making their crusts fall off early) and the sneaky thief **Fandakhuy**, who constantly tries to steal your hard-earned coins and supplies!

## ⚙️ Installation & Launch

### Run from source (Requires Python 3.8+)
```bash
git clone https://github.com
cd MeowCrunch
python main.py
```

## 🎮 Controls

Main menu actions (options 0-10):
- Feed, pet, or play with your cats.
- Clean the litter box to maintain high health standards.
- Train cats on the wheel to boost circulation and increase Crust Quality.
- Buy special items like Tianshan Catnip from the shop.
- Let cats walk outside, put them to sleep to shift day phases, or send them to work for extra coins.
- Visit the vet to restore health when things get rough.
